# Standing context

Read before Phase 1 so the evidence you find and the details you keep are aimed at the right readers.

## Who's writing

Lead ML Engineer at Atlassian, Bengaluru. Twelve-plus years. Prior: Staff MLE at Walmart Labs, Senior Data Scientist at PwC Labs, Data Scientist and DevOps at Software AG, Senior Software Engineer at MathWorks. M.Eng from BITS Pilani.

The arc that matters for credibility: embedded systems, then data science, then MLOps, then ML engineering, then AI leadership. He has seen the field change from before it was called AI.

## Credentials that can be cited in-piece

- 8 patents filed, mostly in alert classification, model explainability, and feedback-driven model improvement
- Publications: AsML 2022 (scalable personalised insights), MLOps quality framework 2023, GIDS 2025 (reducing on-call fatigue with Signal and Noise)
- Atlassian: Big Kudos Award, EngFest demo, CEO keynote mention
- Walmart: Excellence Award

Use these as evidence of first-hand experience, never as credentialing. A specific system detail does more work than a title.

## Systems he can write from

- **SnR (Signal and Noise)**: hybrid alert classification, DBSCAN + XGBoost fused with semantic embeddings, thousands of tenants, feedback overlay A/B with an 11.5pp improvement
- **Rovo Ops Agent**: multi-agent orchestration, eval and routing strategies
- **Provisioner Publisher**: multi-tenant ML training orchestration, region-aware distribution, check-before-claim
- **Alert correlation**: persistent regions and incident detection via temporal clustering
- **Walmart supplier insights**: per-tenant models, 20TB+ training data, $100M+ revenue contribution

Check what is safe to disclose. Keep architectural patterns and methodology, drop anything that identifies internal thresholds, customer data, or unreleased roadmap.

## Audience

Frontier lab engineers, senior calibrators at large tech companies, GenAI startup founders, and senior ML engineers. This audience punishes imprecision and rewards conceding the counterargument.

Consequence: a technical error is far more costly than a dull sentence. Phase 0 exists for this audience.

## Positioning

Hybrid ML systems for enterprise AI. Three rotating sub-tracks:
1. Production ML reliability and evals
2. Multi-agent system design
3. Zero-to-one ML product engineering

Every piece should sit under one of these. The differentiator against the crowded field is scar tissue from shipping, not familiarity with the literature.
